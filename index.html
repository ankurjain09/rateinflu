About TrustReview
TrustReview is a community-driven platform where real customers review courses, coaching, and services from influencers across platforms. We verify signals, surface authentic feedback, and help you choose creators with confidence.

Our mission is to bring transparency to the creator economy. We aggregate verified reviews, highlight track records, and reduce noise so you can focus on signal.

How we verify: community flags, admin review for new submissions, consistency checks across platforms, and optional proof artifacts from reviewers.

Use cases: compare fitness coaches, evaluate finance mentors, shortlist tech bootcamps, or vet marketing consultants — all in one place.
